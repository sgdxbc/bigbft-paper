\section{Conclusion}

In this work, we introduce \sys, a BFT protocol with scalable storage consumption without compromising security.
\sys's state management draws inspiration from distributed databases, while also inheriting the consistent ordering and execution mechanisms of blockchain protocols.
We posit that \sys broadens the potential adoption landscape for both blockchains and databases.
Building upon \sys, several avenues for future research can be pursued, such as developing collaborative databases across mutually distrusting organizations and designing execution models for on-chain big data processing.
